# Tobar

A listening companion for recordings from Tobar an Dualchais and rareTunes.
Save links to recordings, sort them into your own folders and tags, rate them,
make playlists, and keep your own notes and lyrics. Everything syncs between
your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud. You can also record songs,
tunes and interviews of your own and prepare them, with a consent form, to
offer to an archive.

Tobar is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tobar an
Dualchais or rareTunes.
