# Privacy Policy — Tobar

*Last updated: 3 October 2026*

Tobar collects nothing.

There is no Tobar account, no Tobar server and no sign-in. Nobody, including
the developer, can see what you save or write in the app.

## What the app stores, and where

Your bookmarks (links to recordings and pages), your folders, tags, star
ratings, playlists, notes and lyrics are kept in a database inside the app on
your device. So are the descriptions of any contributions you prepare: titles,
the names of performers and collectors, places, summaries, consent details and
similar information you type in.

## iCloud

This information also goes to **your own iCloud account**, so the same library
is on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. This happens automatically when you are signed
in to iCloud on the device. It goes into your account's *private* database,
which Apple encrypts, which the developer cannot see or ask for, and which no
other user of Tobar can reach.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, or you turn iCloud off for Tobar in your
device's Settings, the app keeps working and everything stays on that device.
If you have iCloud Backup switched on, your device may include the app's data in
your own backup. Both are Apple's services under your own Apple Account, covered
by Apple's privacy policy rather than this one.

## Recordings and forms you make yourself

If you record a contribution, or import audio, the sound file is kept **only
on the device that made it**. It is not uploaded to iCloud or anywhere else by
the app. The same goes for photos or scans of signed consent forms.

Tobar asks for the **microphone** only when you start to record, and for the
**camera** only when you choose to scan a signed form (iPhone and iPad). Neither
is used at any other time. If you pick a photo from your library, only the photo
you choose is shared with the app.

When you prepare a contribution to offer to an archive, Tobar assembles a folder
of files on your device. The app sends nothing anywhere itself: you choose
whether to share it, and where, through the standard share sheet or by saving it
to a folder.

## Tobar an Dualchais and rareTunes

Tobar shows pages from **tobarandualchais.co.uk** and **raretunes.org** in a
web view inside the app, unmodified, and plays recordings on those pages. It
does not download or copy any audio. When you browse, your device connects to
those websites in the ordinary way, as it would in a browser. What those sites
collect, including through cookies, is for them to describe. Nothing about you
is added to the request by Tobar.

A bookmark stores the address of a page, its title, and what you add yourself.
Tobar has no access to the other information on those sites beyond what is shown
on the page in front of you.

Links on those pages to other websites open in your browser. At that point you
have left Tobar.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting of any kind.
- No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
- No tracking, and no sharing of anything with any other company.
- No accounts, and no email address or name collected by the app.
- No location.
- No third-party SDKs.

## Children

Tobar has no accounts, no messaging, no content visible to anyone else, and no
purchases. It collects nothing from anyone of any age. If you collect
recordings from a child or young person, the consent form includes a line for a
parent or guardian to sign.

## Changes

If this ever stops being true, the policy will be updated before the change
ships, and the date at the top will say when.
